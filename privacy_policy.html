OKADA CO., LTD. ("we," "us," or "our") operates as a seller on eBay, specializing in vintage vinyl records. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information in connection with our eBay selling activities and any related online services.

1. Information We Collect

When you purchase an item from us through eBay, or otherwise contact us in connection with a transaction, we may receive the following information, either directly from you or through the eBay platform:

  • Name and shipping address
  • Email address and, where provided, phone number
  • Order details (items purchased, price paid, order date)
  • Payment confirmation status (processed by eBay Managed Payments; we do not receive or store your full payment card or bank details)
  • Correspondence you send us regarding an order

2. How We Use This Information

We use this information solely to:

  • Process and fulfill your order
  • Arrange shipping and provide tracking information
  • Respond to your questions or resolve issues with an order
  • Comply with legal, tax, and customs requirements

We do not use this information for unsolicited marketing.

3. Third-Party Disclosure

We share information only as necessary to complete a transaction:

  • With eBay, as the marketplace platform through which the sale occurs
  • With shipping carriers (such as FedEx or DHL) to deliver your order and provide tracking
  • With customs authorities, where required for international shipments
  • With professional advisors (e.g., accountants) or government authorities, where required by law

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for their own marketing purposes.

4. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the information we hold from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or loss. Information is retained only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described above or to meet legal obligations (e.g., tax record retention).

5. Your Rights

You may contact us at any time to ask what information we hold about you, to request a correction, or to request deletion of information we are not otherwise required to retain.
